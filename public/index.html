About TeamGoo

A blockchain-based ticket marketplace designed to make buying, owning, and reselling event tickets more transparent and verifiable.

The Problem

Traditional ticket marketplaces face challenges such as counterfeit tickets, duplicate sales, and uncertainty about ticket ownership. Buyers often depend on centralized platforms to confirm whether a ticket is legitimate.

Our Solution

TeamGoo provides a marketplace where authorized organizations can issue digital tickets, buyers can purchase them, and ticket owners can list them for resale.

Ticket ownership and transactions are recorded on the DIDLab blockchain, allowing the marketplace to verify ticket information against smart contract records.

Why Blockchain?

Blockchain provides a shared, tamper-resistant history of ticket transactions. Rather than relying only on information stored in a marketplace database, ticket ownership and transfers can be checked against blockchain records.

How TeamGoo Works

  1. Ticket Issuance: Authorized organizations create events and mint digital tickets using a smart contract.
  2. Ticket Purchase: Buyers connect their MetaMask wallet and purchase available tickets through the marketplace.
  3. Ownership Verification: The smart contract records ticket ownership, allowing users to verify who holds a ticket.
  4. Ticket Resale: Ticket owners can list tickets for resale, and ownership is updated after a successful purchase.

On-Chain vs. Off-Chain Data

TeamGoo uses a hybrid approach to avoid storing unnecessary information on the blockchain.

On-chain: Ticket identifiers, ownership, prices, transaction records, and ticket status.

Off-chain: Organization names, event descriptions, venues, dates, and associations between events and tickets.

Technology

TeamGoo uses Solidity smart contracts deployed on DIDLab, MetaMask for wallet interactions, and ethers.js to communicate with the blockchain. An Express backend and SQLite database manage event and organization information.
